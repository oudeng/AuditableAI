# Four supporting implementations

This dissertation is an integration of complementary work, with additional application experiments. Original training pipelines stay in the four study repositories. This keeps their separate software environments and evidence conventions explicit.

| Study | Official repository | Dissertation chapter | Package contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| RPS | [oudeng/RPS](https://github.com/oudeng/RPS) | 4 | Mechanism/certificate illustrations and an optional redraw of 18 reported family means |
| LGO | [oudeng/LGO](https://github.com/oudeng/LGO) | 5 | Gate-function illustrations, published component curves and the basis for the new additive estimator |
| VERA | [oudeng/VERA](https://github.com/oudeng/VERA) | 6 | Same-host mechanism, released faithfulness summaries and exact reaggregation of five seed blocks |
| CaST | [oudeng/CaST](https://github.com/oudeng/CaST) | 7 | Protocol/fusion schematics and selected final-system aggregate retrieval results |

Repository identities inspected on 2026-10-04 are recorded with commit SHAs in [`upstream_repositories.json`](../provenance/upstream_repositories.json). Those SHAs identify what was consulted for repository presentation; they do **not** retroactively identify the development versions behind all dissertation results.

## Presentation choices adopted

- LGO/RPS: a short purpose statement, executable quick start, directory map and experiment parameters.
- VERA: separate software/evidence layers, explicit omission of restricted derivatives, clear interpretation of content hashes.
- CaST: separate table/figure regeneration from end-to-end training, identify source versions, explain reproduction pitfalls.

The packaging retains the thesis-specific limitations, rather than importing claims or historical status from another README. VERA's account of its own AI-assisted commits is not reused as a claim about this repository.
